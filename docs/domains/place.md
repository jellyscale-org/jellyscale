# Place

One history per person and title, and what "played", "continue" and "next episode" mean.

## Words

- **Place:** where a person stopped in a title.
- **Played:** the person has watched it.
- **Continue watching:** titles in progress and each show's next unplayed episode.
- **Next episode:** what follows the last one a person played in a show.

## Must not

Know devices, playbacks or HTTP. Show one person another's history.

## Rules

1. One history per profile and title, whichever device or version was watched. Only Place writes it.
2. An older or late report never undoes a newer one.
3. Taking a title off Continue watching keeps its place; it returns when watched again.
4. Continue watching is the same on every device.
