# Finding

Every read of titles for a viewer: Films, Shows, details, search, filters and lists.

## Words

- **Home:** the short first screen, the same on every device.
- **My list:** titles a viewer saved.
- **Collection:** a set of titles of one kind: a franchise in watch order, a list someone made, a saved rule.
- **Filter:** a fact a viewer narrows a list by, such as unwatched, language, 4K or HDR.

## Must not

Read a title without a viewer; write a title; rank by other people's viewing.

## Rules

1. No title is read for a viewer except through that viewer's access. Lists, counts, positions, search, suggestions, Continue watching and next episode all go through it.
2. A viewer is never shown that something they may not see exists, counts included.
3. Nothing is ranked or hidden by what other people watch. A suggestion comes from the titles themselves and from the viewer, and shows its reason.
4. Films and Shows hold everything the viewer may see, however the operator keeps the files.
5. Lists filter and sort on stored facts. An answer is shaped at the edge.
