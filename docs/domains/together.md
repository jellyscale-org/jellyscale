# Together

Groups watching in step, and moving a film between devices. Last on the road (PRODUCT.md).

## Words

- **Group:** people watching the same title in step.

## Must not

Give a member anything Playback would refuse them alone. Need every member on the same server process.

## Rules

1. A group holds playbacks. Each member's playback passed access and limits by itself.
2. Moving a film to another device starts a new playback from the old one's title, position and picks, checked for access and limits again. The old one ends.
3. Nothing here weakens a rule of Playback or People.
